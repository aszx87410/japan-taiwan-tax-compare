# Taiwan-Japan Tax Comparison Calculator

Estimate after-tax salary income in Taiwan and Japan using official data available for 2026. The calculator covers Taiwan income tax, NHI, labor insurance, employment insurance, Japan income tax, Tokyo resident tax, health insurance, employees' pension, and employment insurance.

## Canonical page

https://aszx87410.github.io/japan-taiwan-tax-compare/en.html

## Supported scenarios

- Single
- Married, no child
- Married, one 3-year-old child

## Calculations covered

- Income tax
- Resident tax
- Health insurance
- Labor insurance
- Employment insurance
- Health insurance
- Employees' pension
- Employment insurance

## Assumptions

- Figures are based on official data available for 2026. Actual liabilities still depend on government rules and individual filing details.
- Spouse income is assumed to be 0.
- When monthly income is below 88,000 JPY, this simplified model treats the worker as below the short-time-worker eligibility threshold for health insurance and employees' pension. This wage requirement is scheduled to be abolished in October 2026.
